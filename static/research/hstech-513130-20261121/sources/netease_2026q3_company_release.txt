( NTES and 9999 , "NetEase" or the "Company"), a leading internet and game services provider, today announced its unaudited financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026. Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights Net revenues were RMB27.9 billion (US$4.2 billion), a decrease of 1.8% compared with the same quarter of 2025. Games and related value-added services net revenues were RMB22.9 billion (US$3.4 billion), a decrease of 1.7% compared with the same quarter of 2025. Youdao net revenues were RMB1.4 billion (US$213.1 million), a decrease of 12.3% compared with the same quarter of 2025. NetEase Cloud Music net revenues were RMB1.9 billion (US$290.3 million), a slight decrease of 0.9% compared with the same quarter of 2025. Innovative businesses and others net revenues were RMB1.5 billion (US$229.7 million), an increase of 7.1% compared with the same quarter of 2025. Gross profit was RMB19.0 billion (US$2.8 billion), an increase of 4.8% compared with the same quarter of 2025. Total operating expenses were RMB9.6 billion (US$1.4 billion), a decrease of 5.4% compared with the same quarter of 2025. Net income attributable to the Company's shareholders was RMB8.4 billion (US$1.3 billion). Non-GAAP net income attributable to the Company's shareholders was RMB9.2 billion (US$1.4 billion).[1] Basic net income per share was US$0.39 (US$1.96 per ADS). Non-GAAP basic net income per share was US$0.43 (US$2.13 per ADS).[1] [1] As used in this announcement, non-GAAP net income attributable to the Company's shareholders and non-GAAP basic and diluted net income per share and per ADS are defined to exclude share-based compensation expenses. See the unaudited reconciliation of GAAP and non-GAAP results at the end of this announcement. Third Quarter 2026 and Recent Operational Highlights Sustained engagement across established titles, underscoring the lasting appeal of our gaming portfolio. The Fantasy Westward Journey franchise, Identity V , Eggy Party , Sword of Justice and Where Winds Meet continued to captivate diverse player communities through content updates, gameplay innovation and crossover collaborations. Strengthened our global appeal through differentiated gaming experiences and continued content innovation. Marvel Rivals deepened engagement with players worldwide through new features and fresh content, while Where Winds Meet surpassed 100 million players globally in August 2026, highlighting our growing capabilities in game development and live operations for the global market. Advanced our diverse pipeline of games spanning more genres, gameplay experiences and themes. We progressed development and launch preparations for Ananta , which is currently scheduled for a multiplatform global launch on January 15, 2027. We also began rolling out player tests for several other titles in development. "In the third quarter, we remained committed to delivering thoughtfully crafted game content and nurturing lasting player communities through fresh and compelling experiences," said Mr. William Ding, Chief Executive Officer and Director of NetEase. "While we continue to evolve our established titles with content and gameplay mechanics that appeal to a broader range of players, we are also steadily advancing our titles in development across genres, platforms and markets. "Looking ahead, we remain focused on bringing together creative vision, technological innovation and strong execution to deliver games that stand the test of time. By continuing to strengthen our development and live operations capabilities, we aim to bring exceptional gaming experiences to vibrant player communities worldwide, creating long-term value for our partners and shareholders," Mr. Ding concluded. Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results Net Revenues Net revenues for the third quarter of 2026 were RMB27.9 billion (US$4.2 billion), compared with RMB30.1 billion and RMB28.4 billion for the preceding quarter and the same quarter of 2025, respectively. Net revenues from games and related value-added services were RMB22.9 billion (US$3.4 billion) for the third quarter of 2026, compared with RMB25.0 billion and RMB23.3 billion for the preceding quarter and the same quarter of 2025, respectively. Net revenues from the operation of online games accounted for approximately 97.5% of the segment's net revenues for the third quarter of 2026, compared with 97.7% and 97.6% for the preceding quarter and the same quarter of 2025, respectively. The quarter-over-quarter decrease was primarily due to lower net revenues from certain self-developed games. Net revenues from Youdao were RMB1.4 billion (US$213.1 million) for the third quarter of 2026, compared with RMB1.5 billion and RMB1.6 billion for the preceding quarter and the same quarter of 2025, respectively. The year-over-year decrease was mainly due to decreased net revenues from its smart devices and online marketing services. Net revenues from NetEase Cloud Music were RMB1.9 billion (US$290.3 million) for the third quarter of 2026, compared with RMB2.0 billion each for the preceding quarter and the same quarter of 2025. Net revenues from innovative businesses and others were RMB1.5 billion (US$229.7 million) for the third quarter of 2026, compared with RMB1.6 billion and RMB1.4 billion for the preceding quarter and the same quarter of 2025, respectively. Cost of Revenues Cost of revenues for the third quarter of 2026 was RMB8.8 billion (US$1.3 billion), compared with RMB8.9 billion and RMB10.2 billion for the preceding quarter and the same quarter of 2025, respectively. The year-over-year decrease was mainly due to lower revenue-sharing costs. Gross Profit Gross profit for the third quarter of 2026 was RMB19.0 billion (US$2.8 billion), compared with RMB21.2 billion and RMB18.2 billion for the preceding quarter and the same quarter of 2025, respectively. Operating Expenses Total operating expenses for the third quarter of 2026 were RMB9.6 billion (US$1.4 billion), compared with RMB9.1 billion and RMB10.2 billion for the preceding quarter and the same quarter of 2025, respectively. The quarter-over-quarter increase was primarily due to increased marketing expenses. The year-over-year decrease was primarily due to decreased marketing expenses and general and administrative expenses. Other Income/(Expenses) Other income/(expenses) consisted of investment income/(loss), interest income, net exchange gains/(losses) and others. The quarter-over-quarter and year-over-year fluctuations in other income/(expenses) were mainly due to fair value changes of listed equity securities. Income Tax The Company recorded a net income tax charge of RMB1.9 billion (US$275.9 million) for the third quarter of 2026, compared with RMB2.5 billion and RMB1.3 billion for the preceding quarter and the same quarter of 2025, respectively. The effective tax rate for the third quarter of 2026 was 17.8%, compared with 25.5% and 13.0% for the preceding quarter and the same quarter of 2025, respectively. The effective tax rate represents certain estimates by the Company as to the tax obligations and benefits applicable to it in each quarter. Net Income and Non-GAAP Net Income Net income attributable to the Company's shareholders totaled RMB8.4 billion (US$1.3 billion) for the third quarter of 2026, compared with RMB7.0 billion and RMB8.6 billion for the preceding quarter and the same quarter of 2025, respectively. Basic net income was US$0.39 per share (US$1.96 per ADS) for the third quarter of 2026, compared with US$0.33 per share (US$1.63 per ADS) and US$0.40 per share (US$2.01 per ADS) for the preceding quarter and the same quarter of 2025, respectively. Non-GAAP net income attributable to the Company's shareholders totaled RMB9.2 billion (US$1.4 billion) for the third quarter of 2026, compared with RMB7.7 billion and RMB9.5 billion for the preceding quarter and the same quarter of 2025, respectively. Non-GAAP basic net income was US$0.43 per share (US$2.13 per ADS) for the third quarter of 2026, compared with US$0.36 per share (US$1.80 per ADS) and US$0.44 per share (US$2.22 per ADS) for the preceding quarter and the same quarter of 2025, respectively. Other Financial Information As of September 30, 2026, the Company's net cash (total cash and cash equivalents, current and non-current time deposits and restricted cash, as well as short-term investments balance, minus loans) totaled RMB175.2 billion (US$26.1 billion), compared with RMB163.5 billion as of December 31, 2025. Net cash provided by operating activities was RMB10.5 billion (US$1.6 billion) for the third quarter of 2026, compared with RMB10.0 billion and RMB12.9 billion for the preceding quarter and the third quarter of 2025, respectively. Non-GAAP Financial Measures NetEase considers and uses non-GAAP financial measures, such as non-GAAP net income attributable to the Company's shareholders and non-GAAP basic and diluted net income per ADS and per share, as supplemental metrics in reviewing and assessing its operating performance and formulating its business plan. The presentation of non-GAAP financial measures is not intended to be considered in isolation or as a substitute for the financial information prepared and presented in accordance with accounting principles generally accepted in the United States of America ("U.S. GAAP"). NetEase defines non-GAAP net income attributable to the Company's shareholders as net income attributable to the Company's shareholders excluding share-based compensation expenses. Non-GAAP net income attributable to the Company's shareholders enables NetEase's management to assess its operating results without considering the impact of share-based compensation expenses. NetEase believes that this non-GAAP financial measure provides useful information to investors in understanding and evaluating the Company's current operating performance and prospects in the same manner as management does, if they so choose. NetEase also believes that the use of this non-GAAP financial measure facilitates investors' assessment of its operating performance. Non-GAAP financial measures are not defined under U.S. GAAP and are not presented in accordance with U.S. GAAP. Non-GAAP financial measures have limitations as analytical tools. One of the key limitations of using non-GAAP net income attributable to the Company's shareholders is that it does not reflect all items of expense/ income that affect our operations. Share-based compensation expenses have been and may continue to be incurred in NetEase's business and are not reflected in the presentation of non-GAAP net income attributable to the Company's shareholders. In addition, the non-GAAP financial measures NetEase uses may differ from the non-GAAP measures used by other companies, including peer companies, and therefore their comparability may be limited. NetEase compensates for these limitations by reconciling non-GAAP net income attributable to the Company's shareholders to the nearest U.S. GAAP performance measure, all of which should be considered when evaluating the Company's performance. See the unaudited reconciliation of GAAP and non-GAAP results at the end of this announcement.
