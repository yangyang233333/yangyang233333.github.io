TENCENT ANNOUNCES 2026 THIRD QUARTER RESULTS 
Advancing across Models and Applications  
Accelerating Evolution for the New AI Era  
 
Hong Kong, 18 November 2026 – Tencent Holdings Limited (HKEX: 00700 (HKD Counter) and 80700 (RMB 
Counter), “Tencent” or “the Company”), a world-leading Internet and technology company in China, today 
announced the unaudited consolidated results for the quarter ended 30 September 2026 (“3Q2026”). 
 
Mr. Ma Huateng, Chairman and CEO of Tencent, said, “Our recent significant achievements across models 
and applications have accelerated Tencent’s evolution for the new AI era. Hy4 has achieved China state-of-
the-art AI model status, and Hy4 flash brings down inference costs to deliver cost performance for real-world 
use cases. We believe WorkBuddy has grown to become the largest AI application in China, measured by 
total daily large language model token consumption by users. Our cloud services revenue growth rate 
increased, driven by WorkBuddy and as we allocated more compute to Model as a Service and GPU rental. 
Our core businesses remained resilient and vibrant as several evergreen games reached lifetime highs in 
DAU, and as Video Accounts increased engagement rapidly, particularly among young users, by enriching its 
content offering.”  
 
3Q2026 Financial Highlights 
Revenues: +8% YoY, gross profit: +7% YoY, non-IFRS operating profit: -6% YoY 
 Total revenues were RMB207.3 billion, up 8% over the third quarter of 2025 (“YoY”).  
 Gross profit was RMB115.9 billion, up 7% YoY. 
 On a non-IFRS basis, which is intended to reflect core earnings by excluding certain one-time and/or 
non-cash items: 
- Operating profit was RMB68.1 billion, down 6% YoY. Operating margin decreased to 33% from 
38% last year. 
- Operating profit excluding New AI Products1  was RMB83.3 billion, increased by 8% YoY. 
Operating margin excluding new AI products contributions was 40%, stable from last year.  
- Net profit was RMB 68.4 billion, down 6% YoY.  
- Net profit attributable to equity holders of the Company was RMB66.5 billion, down 6% YoY.  
- Basic earnings per share were RMB7.382. Diluted earnings per share were RMB7.227. 
 On an IFRS basis: 
- Operating profit was RMB59.1 billion, down 7% YoY. Operating margin decreased to 29% from 
33% last year.  
- Net profit was RMB65.1 billion, up 0.3% YoY.  
- Net profit attributable to equity holders of the Company was RMB63.6 billion, up 0.8% YoY. 
- Basic earnings per share were RMB7.059. Diluted earnings per share were RMB6.947. 
 Capital expenditure payments was RMB65.1 billion, up 225% YoY. 
 Total cash was RMB525.7 billion, up 7% YoY. Net debt position totalled RMB13.7 billion.

 We recorded negative free cash flow of RMB69.1 billion for 3Q2026, as net cash generated from operating 
activities of RMB2.6 billion was more than offset by capital expenditure payments of RMB65.1 billion, 
media content payments of RMB4.6 billion and lease liability payments of RMB2.0 billion. Included in our 
operating cash flow were significant prepayments for compute procurement to support enhancements to 
our Hy model family, WorkBuddy inference needs, and development of AI capabilities across our products 
and services, as well as to meet external demand for our cloud services. Excluding the prepayments for 
compute procurement, our free cash flow would have been positive RMB16.9 billion.  
 The fair value of our shareholdings 2  in listed investee companies (excluding subsidiaries) was 
RMB505.4 billion as at 30 September 2026, compared with RMB487.2 billion as at 30 June 2026. The 
carrying book value of our shareholdings in unlisted investee companies (excluding subsidiaries) 
was RMB397.2 billion as at 30 September 2026, compared with RMB387.9 billion as at 30 June 2026.  
 During 3Q2026, the Company repurchased approximately 15.9 million shares on the Hong Kong Stock 
Exchange for an aggregate consideration of approximately HKD7.0 billion. 
 
3Q2026 Management Discussion and Analysis 
Revenues from VAS increased by 3% year-on-year to RMB98.3 billion for 3Q2026. Domestic Games revenues 
were RMB47.6 billion, up 11% year-on-year, driven by growth in revenues from games including Delta Force, 
VALORANT MOBILE and Honour of Kings. International Games revenues were RMB18.9 billion, down 9% 
year-on-year (or down 4% on a constant currency basis), reflecting a high base period arising from social 
media excitement around certain Supercell games and from copy sales of Dying Light: The Beast released in 
the year-ago period. Social Networks revenues decreased by 1% year-on-year to RMB31.8 billion. 
 
Revenues from Marketing Services rose by 19% year-on-year to RMB43.0 billion for 3Q2026. We enhanced 
our AI-driven ad and content recommendation capabilities and introduced an AI-powered ad solution for 
content publishers that automates ad insertion and optimises placement timing and duration. Within the Weixin 
ecosystem, we deepened the integration of closed-loop marketing capabilities, enabling Mini Dramas, Mini 
Shops and other Weixin-integrated services to advertise more effectively within Weixin and other Tencent-
managed ad properties. Major industry categories, notably consumer goods and local services, increased 
marketing spending on our platforms during the quarter. 
 
Revenues from FinTech and Business Services rose by 9% year-on-year to RMB63.7 billion for 3Q2026. 
FinTech Services revenue growth arose from higher revenues from consumer loan and wealth management 
services. Business Services revenue growth was primarily driven by a 35% year-on-year increase in cloud 
services revenues, underpinned by increased demand for AI-related services and continued international 
expansion.

Business Review and Outlook  
 In November, we released the Hy4 model, with significant advances in foundational capabilities and deep 
model-product co-design, unlocking a broad range of real-world productivity use cases. Hy4 ranked first4 
among Chinese models at launch by Agent Arena, demonstrating superior real-world capabilities across 
signals such as task success, user satisfaction, and steerability. 
 WorkBuddy became the most used AI product in China, measured by large language model token 
consumption, with over 10 million DAU collectively consuming over 100 trillion tokens per day, and rapidly 
increasing enterprise adoption. 
 We expanded Xiaowei’s availability and use cases, and are deepening its integration with Mini Programs 
and Mini Shops to facilitate transactional activity. 
 Video Accounts boosted engagement among young users through deeper collaboration with our internal 
game, music and TV show content, contributing to over 20% year-on-year increase in total time spent5. 
 Delta Force (released in 2024) and VALORANT MOBILE (released in 2025) each achieved lifetime high 
DAUs5, demonstrating our ability to expand our new games’ popularity beyond their launch dates, as well 
as players’ enthusiasm for emerging and reinvigorated game categories. 
 We launched a payment app, TenPayGo, that enables foreigners traveling in China to make purchases 
using overseas cards and digital wallets, and integrates convenient features such as paying for transit 
rides, handling tax refunds, and AI-powered translation.

About Tencent 
Tencent uses technology to enrich the lives of Internet users.  
 
Our communication and social services, Weixin and QQ, connect users with each other and with digital content 
and services, both online and offline, making their lives more convenient. Our targeted marketing services 
helps advertisers reach out to hundreds of millions of consumers in China. Our FinTech and business services 
support partners’ business growth and assist their digital upgrade.  
 
We invest strategically in AI intelligence, applications and infrastructure, delivering practical utility to users, 
and empowering human creativity and productivity in the AI era. Tencent was founded in Shenzhen, China, 
in 1998. Tencent has been listed on the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong since 2004.

Non-IFRS Financial Measures 
To supplement the consolidated results of the Group (“the Company and its subsidiaries”) prepared in 
accordance with IFRS, certain additional non-IFRS financial measures (in terms of operating profit, operating 
margin, profit for the period, profit attributable to equity holders of the Company, basic EPS and diluted EPS) 
have been presented in this press release. These unaudited non-IFRS financial measures should be 
considered in addition to, not as a substitute for, measures of the Group’s financial performance prepared in 
accordance with IFRS. In addition, these non-IFRS financial measures may be defined differently from similar 
terms used by other companies. 
 
The Company’s management believes that the non-IFRS financial measures provide investors with useful 
supplementary information to assess the performance of the Group’s core operations by excluding certain 
non-cash items and certain impact of investment-related transactions. In addition, non-IFRS adjustments 
include relevant non-IFRS adjustments for the Group’s major associates based on available published 
financials of the relevant major associates, or estimates made by the Company’s management based on 
available information, certain expectations, assumptions and premises.
