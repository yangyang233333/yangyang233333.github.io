Alibaba Group Announces September Quarter 2026 Results and  
Interim Results for the Six Months Ended September 30, 2026 
Hong Kong, China, November 19, 2026 - Alibaba Group Holding Limited (NYSE: BABA and HKEX: 
9988 (HKD Counter) and 89988 (RMB Counter), “Alibaba” or “Alibaba Group”) today announced its 
financial results for the quarter and the six months ended September 30, 2026. 
“Our full-stack AI capabilities continue to improve commercialization and delivered a strong quarter. 
Growth in Alibaba Cloud’s recurring revenue from external customers was 67%, which drove an 
acceleration of overall cloud revenue growth with significantly improving profitability. AI-related product 
revenue delivered triple-digit growth for the thirteenth consecutive quarter. We recently announced 
comprehensive upgrades spanning proprietary chips, supernode architecture, agent-native cloud 
infrastructure, AI models and agents. Notably, T-Head unveiled its next-generation Zhenwu V900 AI chip. 
In our consumption business, Alibaba E-commerce Group’s profitability improved significantly. Quick 
commerce continued to narrow its losses substantially while maintaining market share,” said Eddie Wu, 
Chief Executive Officer of Alibaba Group. “We will continue to bring technological innovations to our 
comprehensive AI stack to meet the strong and diverse AI demands across industries.” 
“This quarter, our business delivered strong growth in both revenue and earnings because of our strategic 
focus on AI + Cloud growth and profit sustainability in Alibaba E-commerce Group. Cloud segment 
revenue growth continued to accelerate, with quality earnings further driving EBITA margin up to 14.5%. 
The e-commerce segment delivered significant EBITA growth, driven by improving unit economics in quick 
commerce and solid growth of marketplace e-commerce,” said Toby Xu, Chief Financial Officer of Alibaba 
Group. “As AI monetization continues to ramp up, we will maintain disciplined investments in AI 
capabilities with a focus on return on invested capital while targeting to achieve positive free cash flow.” 
BUSINESS HIGHLIGHTS 
In the quarter ended September 30, 2026: 
• Revenue was RMB271,519 million (US$40,498 million), an increase of 10% year-over-year. 
• AI Cloud and Compute Services revenue increased by 53% year-over-year. Revenue from 
external customers increased by 64% year-over-year, primarily attributable to a 67% growth in 
recurring revenue from external customers (excluding compute equipment solutions revenue), 
largely driven by the increasing adoption of AI-related products. 
• Customer management revenue decreased by 5% year-over-year. Excluding the contra revenue 
impact from the new business development program, customer management revenue on a like-
for-like basis would have grown by 1% year-over-year.

primarily attributable to the improved operating results in Taobao Instant Commerce and Cloud

BUSINESS AND STRATEGIC UPDATES 
Alibaba E-commerce Group 
During the quarter, we sustained revenue stability with CMR decreasing 5% year-over-year and growing 1% 
on a like-for-like basis if the contra revenue impact from the new business development program were 
excluded. Adjusted EBITA of the group achieved 243% year-over-year growth this quarter, primarily driven 
by a significant loss reduction in the China Quick Commerce business. In addition, our China E-commerce 
business contributed to stable operating profit and the Global Wholesale business delivered strong profits, 
while we reduced losses in the International E-commerce business. 
We have continued to advance our strategic priorities of unlocking significant synergies across our domestic 
and cross-border businesses, and deepening the integration of AI capabilities across our e-commerce 
platforms. Recent progress includes the integration of management across our domestic and international 
wholesale businesses, as well as the launch of QwenWork E-commerce for merchants, an AI product 
powered by our agentic AI capabilities, in October.  
QwenWork E-commerce is integrated into QwenWork, our one-stop AI agent platform, and is designed to 
automate end-to-end merchant workflows, including store operations, business analytics, advertising and 
customer service. Leveraging our existing merchant tools, it connects directly to Taobao and Tmall data and 
will further support data access across other platforms, enabling more seamless cross-platform operations. In 
addition, by leveraging Qwen model family’s advanced multimodal capabilities, we are able to better 
understand complex consumer needs and deliver more relevant product recommendations with knowledge-
based reasoning. These enhanced capabilities have driven a notable increase in general knowledge-related 
queries, creating more opportunities to identify latent consumer demand, facilitate product discovery, and 
drive sales conversion. 
In our China Quick Commerce business, Taobao Instant Commerce continued to improve its unit economics 
quarter-over-quarter while maintaining market share. This reflected higher average order value, as we 
further improved the order mix through an increasing focus on high-value food and non-food categories. 
Meanwhile, Freshippo maintained its robust growth across online and offline channels, driven by deeper

The number of 88VIP members, our highest spending consumer group, continued to increase by double 
digits year-over-year to approximately 66 million as of September 30, 2026. The ARPU of new 88VIP 
members, defined as those who joined the membership program within the past year, increased significantly 
year-over-year during the 11.11 Global Shopping Festival. We remain focused on the growth and retention 
of 88VIP members through enhanced value proposition to our most valued customers. 
Xianyu is China’s leading consumer-to-consumer community and marketplace for idle goods. It makes 
buying and selling more convenient for consumers and small businesses through AI-powered features 
including smart pricing, automated product listing descriptions and precise buyer targeting, as well as 
consignment services. This quarter, Xianyu continued its strong growth momentum, with daily active users 
and paid orders up 23% and 29% year-over-year, respectively. 
AI Cloud and Compute Services 
For the quarter ended September 30, 2026, revenue from AI Cloud and Compute Services was RMB60,973 
million (US$9,094 million), a 53% increase from the same quarter last year. The growth of revenue from 
external customers accelerated to 64% year-over-year. The growth of recurring revenue from external 
customers, which excludes compute equipment solutions revenue, also accelerated to 67% year-over-year. 
In particular, AI-related product revenue continued to show strong momentum, achieving RMB17,918 
million (US$2,673 million) and delivering the thirteenth consecutive quarter of triple-digit year-over-year 
growth. The strong growth reflected broad-based demand for our AI + Cloud services. Beyond training and 
inference workloads at AI labs, customers across the internet, automotive, embodied AI, financial services 
and biotechnology sectors rapidly expanded their use of our cloud services to support innovation and grow 
their business. 
Cloud 
Alibaba Cloud continued to strengthen our market-leading full-stack AI capabilities, as demonstrated by the 
comprehensive upgrades unveiled at the Apsara Conference in September, spanning proprietary chips, 
supernode architecture, agent-native cloud infrastructure, AI models and agents. These advancements across 
our co-designed and integrated stack have accelerated enterprise adoption and deeper integration into core 
business workflows, enabling us to capitalize on increasingly diverse demand across industries. 
Our ability to empower enterprise AI adoption has earned notable recognition in the 2026 Gartner® Magic 
Quadrant™ for Strategic Cloud Platform Services, a report that comprehensively evaluates how cloud 
service providers support the entire enterprise cloud lifecycle. Alibaba Cloud was named a Leader in the 
highest quadrant, making it the first and only Asia-Pacific provider to receive this distinction. 
Chip Design – T-Head Semiconductor (“T-Head”) 
By rapidly advancing its portfolio of proprietary chips and software, T-Head further strengthened the 
infrastructure foundation underpinning our AI capabilities. 
At the Apsara Conference in September, we unveiled the Zhenwu V900, our next-generation AI chip for 
training and inference, with commercial release scheduled for the first quarter of 2027. Built on our 
proprietary parallel computing architecture, the Zhenwu V900 delivers significant performance 
improvements over its predecessor, the Zhenwu M890. We also open-sourced T-Head SAIL, the software 
stack for Zhenwu chips, to simplify application migration, expand developer adoption, and strengthen 
compatibility with mainstream AI frameworks.

In addition, we launched the Zhenwu M890 supernode instance in August, marking the introduction of 
China’s first supernode architecture capable of supporting inference for models with more than two trillion 
parameters. At the Apsara Conference, we also showcased our next-generation supernode product, which 
integrates the Zhenwu V900 with other proprietary interconnect, networking, and storage chips, and is 
scheduled for launch in the first quarter of 2027.   
AI Labs and Applications 
Model 
We continued to advance our comprehensive model portfolio, with a primary focus on foundation models, 
while enhancing capabilities across video, audio, image and music. This demonstrates our industry 
leadership and rapid pace of iteration.  
Recently, we launched Qwen4-Max, built on the new mixture-of-experts architecture with 2.4 trillion 
backbone parameters and native visual capabilities. Qwen4-Max advances productivity and token efficiency, 
delivering stronger long-horizon task execution across industries, including manufacturing, game 
development, healthcare, and frontier scientific research.  
QwenWork 
Our agentic platform QwenWork continued to gain traction across industries, driven by ongoing 
enhancements to its enterprise capabilities. At the Apsara Conference in September, we launched Enterprise 
Context, a solution that continuously organizes and updates enterprise information and knowledge across 
company documents, meeting records and internal communications. It enables AI agents to access company-
specific information and execute tasks based on organizational knowledge and workflows.  
Qwen App 
Qwen app for individual users now provides personalized assistance for tasks across more than 20 service 
categories, including shopping, local services, travel, work, learning, personal finance and health, leveraging 
Alibaba’s ecosystem and third-party partnerships. In October, we launched Qwen Go!, a cloud-based 
personal agent embedded within the Qwen app. Qwen Go! autonomously executes multi-step tasks across 
platforms, while retaining contextual information and user preferences over time to deliver increasingly 
personalized assistance across everyday activities. 
All Others 
Our store guide and mapping platform Amap offers consumers one-stop access to services such as 
navigation, to-destination services and ride-hailing. On October 1, 2026, the first day of National Day 
holiday in China, Amap recorded an all-time high of approximately 370 million peak daily active users. 
Leveraging Amap Street Stars, a feature that provides a trust-based rating system to support users’ 
consumption decisions, Amap has strengthened its mindshare in local services across consumption use 
cases, including dining, tourist attractions and seasonal activities. 
Share Repurchases 
During the quarter ended September 30, 2026, we repurchased a total of 17.95 million ordinary shares 
(equivalent to approximately 2.24 million ADSs) for a total of US$221 million. These purchases were made 
in the U.S. market under our share repurchase program.

Capital Expenditures  
During the quarter ended September 30, 2026, capital expenditures were RMB50,477 million (US$7,529 
million), an increase of 60% compared to RMB31,501 million in the same quarter of 2025, due to continued 
investments in AI infrastructure to meet strong and accelerating customer demand. The significant year-
over-year increase was due to an acceleration in scaling compute capacity, higher pricing of a broad range of 
chip components, an increase in CPU-compute capacity driven by anticipated growing customer adoption of 
AI agents, and fluctuations in procurement cycles. 
In addition to training and inference demand from AI Labs, we continue to see robust demand from 
customers in China’s emerging innovation economy, including electric vehicles (EVs), embodied AI, and 
biotechnology. Scale is becoming an increasingly critical competitive advantage in cloud computing, 
providing us with orchestration flexibility, operating efficiency and cost advantages. By investing in 
infrastructure today, we aim to extend our scale leadership, strengthen our supply chain and reinforce our 
long-term competitive position.

NON-GAAP FINANCIAL MEASURES 
 
To supplement our consolidated financial statements, which are prepared and presented in accordance with 
GAAP, we use the following non-GAAP financial measures: for our consolidated results, adjusted EBITDA 
(including adjusted EBITDA margin), adjusted EBITA (including adjusted EBITA margin), non-GAAP net 
income, non-GAAP diluted earnings per share/ADS and free cash flow. For more information on these non-
GAAP financial measures, please refer to the table captioned “Reconciliations of Non-GAAP Measures to 
the Nearest Comparable U.S. GAAP Measures” in this results announcement. 
 
We believe that adjusted EBITDA, adjusted EBITA, non-GAAP net income and non-GAAP diluted 
earnings per share/ADS help identify underlying trends in our business that could otherwise be distorted by 
the effect of certain income or expenses that we include in income from operations, net income and diluted 
earnings per share/ADS. We believe that these non-GAAP measures provide useful information about our 
core operating results, enhance the overall understanding of our past performance and future prospects and 
allow for greater visibility with respect to key metrics used by our management in its financial and 
operational decision-making. We present three different income measures, namely adjusted EBITDA, 
adjusted EBITA and non-GAAP net income in order to provide more information and greater transparency 
to investors about our operating results. 
 
We consider free cash flow to be a liquidity measure that provides useful information to management and 
investors about the amount of cash generated by our business that can be used for strategic corporate 

[PDF PAGE 29]
 
29 
 
 
transactions, including investing in our new business initiatives, making strategic investments and 
acquisitions and strengthening our balance sheet. 
 
Adjusted EBITDA, adjusted EBITA, non-GAAP net income, non-GAAP diluted earnings per share/ADS 
and free cash flow should not be considered in isolation or construed as an alternative to income from 
operations, net income, diluted earnings per share/ADS, cash flows or any other measure of performance or 
as an indicator of our operating performance. These non-GAAP financial measures presented here do not 
have standardized meanings prescribed by U.S. GAAP and may not be comparable to similarly titled 
measures presented by other companies. Other companies may calculate similarly titled measures 
differently, limiting their usefulness as comparative measures to our data. 
 
Adjusted EBITDA represents net income before interest and investment income, net, interest expense, 
other income (expense), net, income tax expenses, share of results of equity method investees, certain non-
cash expenses, consisting of share-based compensation expense, amortization and impairment of intangible 
assets, impairment of goodwill, depreciation and impairment of property and equipment, and operating lease 
cost relating to land use rights, and others (including provision in relation to matters outside the ordinary 
course of business), which we do not believe are reflective of our core operating performance during the 
periods presented. 
 
Adjusted EBITA represents net income before interest and investment income, net, interest expense, other 
income (expense), net, income tax expenses, share of results of equity method investees, certain non-cash 
expenses, consisting of share-based compensation expense, amortization and impairment of intangible 
assets, impairment of goodwill, and others (including provision in relation to matters outside the ordinary 
course of business), which we do not believe are reflective of our core operating performance during the 
periods presented. 
 
Non-GAAP net income represents net income before non-cash share-based compensation expense, 
amortization and impairment of intangible assets, gain or loss on deemed disposals/disposals/revaluation of 
investments, impairment of goodwill and investments, and others (including provision in relation to matters 
outside the ordinary course of business), and adjustments for the tax effects. 
 
Non-GAAP diluted earnings per share represents non-GAAP net income attributable to ordinary 
shareholders divided by the weighted average number of outstanding ordinary shares, in each case for 
computing non-GAAP diluted earnings per share on a diluted basis. Non-GAAP diluted earnings per ADS 
represents non-GAAP diluted earnings per share after adjusting for the ordinary share-to-ADS ratio. 
 
Free cash flow represents net cash provided by operating activities as presented in our consolidated cash 
flow statement less purchases of property and equipment (excluding acquisition of land use rights and 
construction in progress relating to office campuses) and intangible assets (excluding those acquired through 
acquisitions), as well as adjustments to exclude from net cash provided by operating activities the buyer 
protection fund deposits from merchants on our marketplaces. We deduct certain items of cash flows from 
investing activities in order to provide greater transparency into cash flow from our revenue-generating 
business operations. We exclude “acquisition of land use rights and construction in progress relating to 
office campuses” because the office campuses are used by us for corporate and administrative purposes and 
are not directly related to our revenue-generating business operations. We also exclude buyer protection 
fund deposits from merchants on our marketplaces because these deposits are restricted for the purpose of 
compensating buyers for claims against merchants.

Measures” in this results announcement has more details on the non-GAAP financial measures that are most 
directly comparable to GAAP financial measures and the related reconciliations between these financial 
measures.
